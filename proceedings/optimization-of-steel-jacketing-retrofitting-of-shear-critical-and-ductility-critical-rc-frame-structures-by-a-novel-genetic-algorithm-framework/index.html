Optimization of steel-jacketing retrofitting of shear-critical and ductility-critical RC frame structures by a novel genetic algorithm framework

Sberna A.P., Di Trapani Fabio, Marano Giuseppe C
COMPDYN 2021 – 8th ECCOMAS International Conference on Computational Methods in Structural Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering, 28th–30th June 2021 | Athens (Greece) — June 2021
Source

Abstract

A new specific optimization framework directed at minimizing seismic retrofitting intervention costs of reinforced concrete frame structures, providing topological optimization and sizing of steel-jacketing reinforcement.
