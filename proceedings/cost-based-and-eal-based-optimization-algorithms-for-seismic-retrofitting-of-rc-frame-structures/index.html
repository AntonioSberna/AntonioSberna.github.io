Cost-based and EAL based optimization algorithms for seismic retrofitting of RC frame structures

Di Trapani F., Sberna A.P., Tomaselli G., Marano G.C.
Italian Concrete Days 2020, 14th–16th April 2021 | Naples (Italy) — April 2021

Extended in Computers and Structures, 2022

Abstract

Two frameworks for the engineered optimization of seismic retrofitting for reinforced concrete buildings: one directed at minimizing intervention costs, the other based on the assessment of expected annual loss.
