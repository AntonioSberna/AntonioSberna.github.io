Conference Paper

Genetic algorithm-based optimization procedure for the seismic retrofitting of existing masonry structures

Di Trapani Fabio, Sberna A.P., Demartino Cristoforo, Marano Giuseppe C
CST – 14th International Conference on Computational Structures Technology, 23rd–25th August 2022 | Montpellier (France) — August 2022
Source

Abstract

This paper presents a novel optimization framework aimed at the minimization of seismic retrofitting-related costs by pinpointing the optimal position of glass-fibers (GFRP) reinforced plasters in masonry structures.
