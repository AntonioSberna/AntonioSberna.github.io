Dynamic response of infilled frames subject to accidental column losses

Di Trapani F., Tomaselli G., Sberna A.P., Rosso M.M., Marano G.C.
EUROSTRUCT 2021 – 1st Conference of the European Association on Quality Control of Bridges and Structures, 29th August–1st September 2021 | Padua (Italy) — August 2021
Source

Abstract

The paper investigates the role of infilled frames in the dynamic response of a 2D frame subject to an instantaneous column removal case.
