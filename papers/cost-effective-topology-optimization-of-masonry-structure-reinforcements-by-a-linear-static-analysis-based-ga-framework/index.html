Cost-effective topology optimization of masonry structure reinforcements by a linear static analysis-based GA framework

Sberna A.P., Demartino C., Vanzi I., Marano G.C., Di Trapani F.
Bulletin of Earthquake Engineering — June 2024
PDF DOI Source

Conference versions CST 2022 · 3ECEES 2022 · 19th ANIDIS Conference 2022

In short

Many existing buildings stand on masonry walls with no reinforcement, which makes them vulnerable to earthquakes. This study presents a computer method that chooses which walls to strengthen with reinforced plaster, so that the building passes its safety checks at the lowest cost.

Abstract

The paper presents a novel optimization framework aimed at the minimization of seismic retrofitting-related costs for existing unreinforced masonry building structures. The framework provides the topology optimization of the reinforcements (reinforced plasters) to implement in masonry walls for the accomplishment of seismic safety checks.

Highlights

  • A genetic algorithm framework minimizes seismic retrofitting costs of existing unreinforced masonry buildings.
  • Topology optimization locates the reinforced plasters needed in the walls to satisfy seismic safety checks.
  • The algorithm, developed in MATLAB, controls a 3D equivalent frame model built in OpenSees.
  • Each candidate solution is assessed through in-plane shear and flexural safety checks of the walls.
  • A case study with average-quality and poor-quality masonry shows effective cost minimization.
