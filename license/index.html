AI-generated images

Some illustrations are generated with AI tools from my prompts, and each post says so. Their legal status is unclear. In Italy, where this blog is written, copyright protects works created with the help of AI only if they result from the author’s own intellectual work, but the law does not define how much human contribution is enough (an overview in English). The US Copyright Office takes a similar view and considers prompts alone insufficient. These images may therefore not be protected by copyright at all. To the extent that they are, they are released under the same CC BY 4.0 license. I’m no expert on these matters, but some clarity would help, and they are already overdue.
